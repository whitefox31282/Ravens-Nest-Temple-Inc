## Overview

Raven's Nest is a grassroots working refuge and mutual aid infrastructure project. We are building practical, open source alternatives to extractive corporate systems focusing on hands on trade education, sustainable food production, off grid resilience, and community self reliance.

We are not a tech startup or a venture-backed corporation. We are building this from the dirt up, one step at a time, through sweat equity and open collaboration.

## What We Are Building

* Off Grid Infrastructure: Establishing our foundational basecamp on raw land (Tract's Around, San Bois Mountains, OK), utilizing low overhead, resilient systems for power, water, and shelter.
* Trade Education: Practical, hands on training in woodworking, tool maintenance, metal fabrication, basic solar/electrical setup, and food growing.
* Mutual Aid & Community Support: Creating networks of support for unhoused individuals, veterans, and seekers looking to step out of survival mode and into true personal agency.
* Open Source Tooling: Developing transparent, community-owned digital and physical blueprints that any group can adapt and use locally.
