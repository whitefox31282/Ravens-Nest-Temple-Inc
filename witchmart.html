Meetups

WitchMart also meets people in person. That includes festivals, marketplace pop-ups, restaurant pop-ups, and pagan shop pop-ups, and other gatherings of the same kind.

If a listing makes a profit

Anyone who lists here and makes a profit from that listing is asked to donate to Raven's Nest Temple. The gift goes to the Temple’s work: care, food, and the sanctuary. There is no set amount. Listings that do not make money are not asked.
