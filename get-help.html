Get Help

What is real tonight, and who actually has the help

Raven's Nest Temple is not a shelter, a pantry, or a bill-payment office. The lot is not open. Do not come here for a bed, a meal, power, or water. Nothing on the lot is built yet. The lists below are other organizations. They keep their own hours and their own rules. Call them before you travel.

If someone is in danger now

911

Immediate danger, a fire, an overdose, or a crime in progress. This Temple phone is not 911.

988

Mental-health crisis. Call or text 988. Free, 24 hours, in the United States.

Domestic violence

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233. Text START to 88788. Site: thehotline.org.

Veterans

Homeless veterans: 1-877-424-3838. Veterans Crisis Line: call 988, then press 1.

Help in whatever town you are in

These directories are updated by the groups that run them. This page does not copy their town lists, because a copied list goes stale and sends people to closed doors.

211

Call 211. In many places you can also text your ZIP code to 898-211. Food, rent, utilities, shelter referrals, and rides. 211.org.

The Salvation Army, by ZIP code

Search your ZIP, then call that location before you go. Salvation Army location finder.

A food bank near you

Feeding America looks up the regional food bank for your address. Find your local food bank.

Federal starting points

Food help and SNAP: usa.gov/food-help. Homelessness and hunger: usa.gov, get help. Heating and cooling bills: ask 211 for LIHEAP, or call 1-866-674-6327.

If you are in Oklahoma

Food, statewide

The Regional Food Bank of Oklahoma keeps the pantry map: rfbo.org/get-help. Oklahoma City says it does not run pantries or hand out cash: okc.gov, Get help.

Oklahoma City utility help

Oklahoma City Utilities tells customers who need bill help to call the Salvation Army at (405) 246-1100 and ask if they qualify. That is not a promise they will say yes. Utility customer service: (405) 297-2833.

A ride in Oklahoma City

EMBARK: (405) 235-7433. go.embarkok.com.
