Field guide · Mile 0

The threshold

Hekátē stands where the roads meet. She is the crossroads, the threshold, the torch, and the key. This hall is kept in respect for her, and for every traveler who needs a clear and honest path forward.

You are not lost. Pick a mile marker. Read the path. If you have something true to add, share it. If a route needs to be sent elsewhere, ask for the proper section of the site and it will be directed there.

Proposal means we are still talking. Building means hands are on the work. Done means a person can use it. Nothing on this page is finished.

Field guide · Mile 1

The Elders’ Road

Proposal · not built · opened by Rev Jon the Mad Goose Bradley · November 7, 2026 · no replies

Our elders deserve respect, not abuse, and not a system that has refused to fix itself. Raven’s Nest does not lack compassion, and it does not lack honor.

The path, as opened: a five-acre lot. A mortgage of five hundred dollars a month, split among the people who live there. Food and animals raised on that land, so food is not a second bill. Tools built for the elders who ask. A way to the land. A chair. A cart. A tool fitted to their bodies.

The animals are sacred, the same as the elders. They are honored from birth, through life, and through death. On the day a life is given, that animal has a party, the best day of its life, goes to sleep in peace, and passes its energy on.

Nobody signs over their savings or their land to stay. A gift is not a debt. No one here overrides a choice.

Traveler’s warning. This lot is not open. Do not travel for a room. The five acres are the shape of the path, not a door.
