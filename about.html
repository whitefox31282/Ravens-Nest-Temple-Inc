About Raven's Nest Temple

You belong. You are accepted. Pull up a chair.

Who we are

Raven's Nest Temple Inc. is an Oklahoma non-profit. We are building a place where people can eat, rest, learn a trade, and belong without having to earn the right to any of it. We are not here to make people obey. We are here to help people live.

We believe people with different beliefs can share the same table. We believe knowledge should be shared, compassion should be practiced, and power should serve people rather than control them. We believe community is something we build together.

What we believe

  • Honor
  • Community
  • Purpose
  • Belonging
  • Service
  • Abundance for All

What we are building

A place where a person can wake up, walk outside, and learn a real trade with their hands. Gardens thick enough that a family picks the tomatoes. A pond you can fish. Animals honored from birth through death. Elders who keep their savings and their choices. Food and parts at cost plus fifteen percent so the middleman is gone.

WitchMart is the marketplace side of that. The Witches Co-op is where people list skills and services. Hecate's Highways is where ideas get talked through. None of it is finished.

The truth right now

The first lot is not open. The pond, the shops, the animals, and the longhouse are not built yet. Do not come here tonight for a bed, a meal, or a part. Help that exists today is on the Get Help page, or by phone.

We are applying for grants and working as fast as we can. When money and land allow, the work starts. Until then we stay honest about what is real.

Ravens Evermore. We have landed. Opening soon.
