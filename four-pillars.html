Our Work & Four Pillars

The Foundation of Our Sovereignty and Community Action

1. Sanctuary & Safe Harbor

Creating physical and spiritual refuge for those escaping oppressive systems, domestic disruption, or societal marginalization. Our sanctuary provides a safe space centered on mutual respect, personal autonomy, and spiritual freedom.

2. Sovereign Infrastructure

Building independent systems for survival and thriving. From off-grid power generation and clean water sourcing to sustainable food production and resilient dwelling spaces, we lay the groundwork for a future that governments cannot gatekeep.

3. Mutual Aid & Direct Action

Taking care of our own through direct community support. Through initiatives like our WitchMart co-op and "Feed the Homeless" relief operations, we distribute food, gear, and resources directly where they are needed without administrative red tape.

4. Spiritual Autonomy & Community Craft

Fostering traditional crafts, ritual arts, and independent spiritual study. We offer a community space for practitioners to connect, learn, and practice freely without dogma or hierarchical control.

Human Sovereignty — never dominate, never deceive, never override human agency. · Mutual Aid — a people-powered, transparent economy. · Hard Safety Boundaries — no exploitation, no violation of consent, no endangerment of a minor.
