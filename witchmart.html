WitchMart

This is the market. It is not Get Help, and it is not Community Care. Those pages point at doors and at the Temple's own board. WitchMart is the side that sells.

The rule is cost plus fifteen percent. Not the price a company charges because it can. Food, and the parts we can make, at what they cost to raise, process, or print, plus fifteen percent. A store can ask eight dollars for meat that cost a fraction of that to process. WitchMart exists so that price becomes obsolete. When the market is running, a family should not have to pay a corporation's markup to eat.

The old address was witchmart.org. The market now lives here, as part of Raven's Nest Temple Inc.

Nothing is for sale tonight. No vendors are listed. No food is leaving this page. Do not travel for a bag, a cut, or a part. The lot is not open.

As soon as donations and grants land, and the work is real, the market opens and the prices go up on this page. We are applying now. We are building toward it. A listing appears when a person at the Temple has approved it. Not before.
