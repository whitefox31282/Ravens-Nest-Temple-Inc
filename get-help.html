In Crisis? Get Help

We are here. We are listening. No one turns away. We are a newly established grassroots project building mutual aid infrastructure. We are honest about where we are and what we can do right now. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. If you need to talk, need resources, or are looking for people building something different—contact us.

What We Can Do Right Now

🤝 Immediate Peer Support

Talk to humans who have been where you are. No gatekeeping. No forced treatment. Just people who understand what it means to fall through every crack in the system and decide to build something better.

🏗️ Connection to Building Communities

We are establishing refuge sites and mutual aid networks. If you want to be part of building something real—off-grid, sustainable, trauma-informed—we have space for you in this work.

🛣️ Network & Resources

We are building Ravens Roads—a mutual aid network. We can connect you to people and resources. We can help you move safely. We can introduce you to communities that are doing this work.

💬 Honest Conversation

No fundraising pitch. No diagnosis. No forced labels. We talk about what you need, what's possible, and how we can work together to build something that actually works.

What We Are Building Toward

Emergency shelter. Food security. Trauma-informed care. Education. Economic systems that don't exploit you. We are establishing this site by site, step by step, with the people who will live there.

You are not waiting for this to be finished. You can start building now. Contact us. Tell us what you need. Tell us what you can offer. We figure it out together.
