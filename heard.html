Heard, not checked

People pass along what they heard, by city. We have not checked it. It may be wrong. It may still help. Call before you go.

Send one in.

Email revjonbradley@ravensnesttemple.com with the subject “Heard in [city].” Say the Oklahoma city, what you heard, and the date. You do not have to give your name.

Do not name a child. Do not give the street of a domestic violence shelter. Do not give a private person’s home. We post the city, the words, and the date, marked UNCHECKED. We only pull a note that does one of those three things. We do not call it true.
