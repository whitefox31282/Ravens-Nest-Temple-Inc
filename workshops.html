How it works

Come as you are.

  • 1. Join the Nest. Tell us what you want to learn or teach.
  • 2. We announce gatherings. Dates and places go to members first.
  • 3. Show up. Bring curiosity. We'll provide the rest where we can.
  • 4. Pass it on. Teach the next person what you learned.
Safety first

Axes and open fire are real tools. Every session starts with a safety talk, and we'll never ask anyone to use a tool they haven't been shown how to use.

Teach with us

Have a skill to share?

If you can build, grow, cook, craft, sing or guide, we want you at the fire. Tell us what you can teach.
