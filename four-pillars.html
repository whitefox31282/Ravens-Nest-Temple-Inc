Four Pillars

The infrastructure we are building. We are establishing each one step by step, with the people who will use them.

🏡
Raven's Nest Sanctuary
We are acquiring and building: Off-grid land with hobbit-style housing. Solar, water systems, biodigesters. Animals roaming free. Microgreens on every roof. Trauma-informed community where people can heal, learn, and build together. Not a shelter. A home.
🛒
WitchMart
We are establishing: Cost-plus-15% marketplace. Sell what you make. Buy what you need. Keep every penny you earn. No corporate extraction. Direct trade between makers and community. Economics that work for humans, not algorithms.
📚
Mad Goose Show
We are building: Real education. Workshops. Skills. Teachers from many paths. Learn how to live off-grid, build community, heal trauma, organize resistance. Knowledge gatekept by no one. Teaching because we have lived this.
🗺️
Ravens Roads
We are organizing: Mutual aid travel and sanctuary network. Safe passage for those fleeing danger. Community connections. Never stranded. Never alone. Care infrastructure built by people for people.
