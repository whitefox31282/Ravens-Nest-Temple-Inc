Community Marketplace & Sanctuary WitchMart is the community-driven arm of Raven's Nest Temple, dedicated to supporting independent practitioners, local artisans, and practical off-grid resiliency efforts. We believe true spiritual freedom is built on self-reliance, practical skills, and mutual support. Feed the Homeless & Supply Fund Direct action, on the ground. We gather and distribute emergency food, clean water, cold-weather clothing, and hygiene supplies directly to unhoused individuals in our community. Food & Meal Supplies 100% of non-perishable food and meal donations go straight to local food distribution runs.
