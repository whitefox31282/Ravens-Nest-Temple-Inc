We Are Building This

The Reality Right Now

Raven's Nest Temple, Inc. is newly incorporated. We are acquiring our first land parcel. We are building foundational infrastructure step-by-step. We are establishing the first refuge in a network that will eventually span continents.

We are not yet a massive compound. We don't have a 24/7 crisis center. But we are honest, we are moving fast, and we are building something that cannot be stopped because it is built by people who have nothing left to lose and everything to gain.

Why This Matters Right Now

The systems designed to protect you have failed. Governments extract. Corporations exploit. If you are vulnerable, neurodivergent, queer, disabled, formerly unhoused, or traumatized—the world wasn't built for you to survive with dignity.

We are not waiting for permission. We are building infrastructure for sovereignty. Off-grid systems. Trade networks. Shelter. Education. The kind of mutual aid that actually works because we are doing it for each other, not for a paycheck or a reputation.

What We're Establishing

Our first sanctuary site will have off-grid energy, water systems, hobbit-style housing, animals roaming free, and microgreens on every roof. We're building this step-by-step with the people who will live there.

We're establishing WitchMart—a cost-plus-15% marketplace where you keep what you create. We're building Mad Goose Show—education for people the system left behind. We're setting up Ravens Roads—a mutual aid network for safe passage.

This is not hypothetical. We are doing the work. Every dollar goes to land, infrastructure, tools, and people. Every decision is made with the people it affects.

We Are Governed By Ma'at

Truth. Justice. Harmony. Balance. We don't hide who we are or what we're doing. We make decisions by consensus. We protect the vulnerable. We share what we build.

This is not charity. This is not an NGO that manages poverty. This is mutual aid from the ground up. We invite you to build with us—not as beneficiaries of someone else's vision, but as architects of a future that actually works.
