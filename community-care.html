Food banks by city, Oklahoma

These are public food desks, not Temple partners. Call before you go.

  • 211 statewide: 211 statewide.
  • Tulsa: Food Bank of Eastern Oklahoma, 1304 N Kenosha Ave, 918-585-2800, Mon–Thu 8–5, Fri 8–noon, pantry search okfoodbank.org/find-food.
  • McAlester: 2119 N Main St, 918-429-7755, same hours.
  • Oklahoma City: Regional Food Bank, 3355 S Purdue Ave, call or text 405-972-1111, Mon–Fri 8–5, zip finder rfbo.org/get-help.
  • Moore: 2635 N Shields Blvd, 405-600-3182, Mon 12:30–5:30, Tue closed, Wed–Fri 10–3, third Saturday 9:30–11:30.
  • Norman: The Share Center, 213 Reed Ave, 405-701-1890, Mon closed, Tue–Wed 2:30–5:30, Thu–Fri 11–2, third Saturday 12–2, ID and proof of residency, Norman, Noble, Lexington, or McClain County.
  • Lawton: Lawton Food Bank, 1819 SW Sheridan Rd, 580-353-7994, food Mon 1–5:30, Tue closed, Wed–Fri 10–2, closed federal holidays.
  • Enid: Loaves & Fishes, 701 E Maine Ave, no phone, say call the number on the door.
