Raven's Nest Temple

“Whoa... hey there, traveler. Take a breath, man...” Welcome to Raven's Nest. Nobody is here to override your choices, and this sanctuary is still in the planning stage.

We're building a grassroots refuge from the dirt up: a place to learn trades, grow food, and find a community that has your back—without corporate middlemen or a catch.

We build sanctuary where system reliance ends.

Raven’s Nest Temple Inc. is an Oklahoma non-profit sanctuary dedicated to true mutual aid, personal sovereignty, and off-grid community infrastructure. Shelter, clean water, power, and food should be basic human realities, not commodities.
