WitchMart Sanctuary & Cooperative

Connecting community craft, off-grid self-reliance, and direct mutual aid without administrative overhead.

Community Marketplace & Sanctuary

WitchMart is the community-driven arm of Raven's Nest Temple, dedicated to supporting independent practitioners, local artisans, and practical off-grid resiliency efforts. We believe true spiritual freedom is built on self-reliance, practical skills, and mutual support.

Feed the Homeless & Supply Fund

Direct action, on the ground. We gather and distribute emergency food, clean water, cold-weather clothing, and hygiene supplies directly to unhoused individuals in our community.

Food & Meal Supplies

100% of non-perishable food and meal donations go straight to local food distribution runs.

Survival Gear & Utilities

Providing tents, blankets, solar chargers, and basic off-grid emergency gear during extreme weather.
