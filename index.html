Raven's Nest Temple is a grassroots community project focused on practical skills, growing food, and mutual aid. We are building this step by step, with honesty about where things stand today.

What we are building

A refuge rooted in community and hands-on learning: trade skills, food growing, resilient infrastructure, and ways to support one another. The work is underway one step at a time.

A note about right now

Raven's Nest Temple, Inc. is still in progress. The first lot is not open, and the planned facilities are not built yet. Please do not travel here expecting a bed, meal, or on-site services.
