Food banks by city, Oklahoma

These are public food desks, not Temple partners. Call before you go.

  • 211 statewide: 211 statewide.
  • Tulsa: Food Bank of Eastern Oklahoma, 1304 N Kenosha Ave, 918-585-2800, Mon–Thu 8–5, Fri 8–noon, pantry search okfoodbank.org/find-food.
  • McAlester: 2119 N Main St, 918-429-7755, same hours.
  • Oklahoma City: Regional Food Bank, 3355 S Purdue Ave, call or text 405-972-1111, Mon–Fri 8–5, zip finder rfbo.org/get-help.
  • Moore: 2635 N Shields Blvd, 405-600-3182, Mon 12:30–5:30, Tue closed, Wed–Fri 10–3, third Saturday 9:30–11:30.
  • Norman: The Share Center, 213 Reed Ave, 405-701-1890, Mon closed, Tue–Wed 2:30–5:30, Thu–Fri 11–2, third Saturday 12–2, ID and proof of residency, Norman, Noble, Lexington, or McClain County.
  • Lawton: Lawton Food Bank, 1819 SW Sheridan Rd, 580-353-7994, food Mon 1–5:30, Tue closed, Wed–Fri 10–2, closed federal holidays.
  • Enid: Loaves & Fishes, 701 E Maine Ave, no phone, say call the number on the door.

Ardmore, Durant, Edmond, Elk City, Ponca City, Shawnee, Stillwater, Weatherford, and Woodward have a Food & Resource Center. Enter the zip first. Do not drive on a guess.

Shelters by town, Oklahoma

A listing is not a bed, and call first.

Statewide: call or text 1-800-522-7233. For a general bed, call 211.

  • Tulsa Day Center: 415 W Archer St, 918-583-5588. Daytime is open to everyone. Night shelter is women and men 55 and older.
  • John 3:16 men overnight: 506 N Cheyenne Ave, 918-587-1187. Arrive by 5pm. Photo ID. Sober. No weapons, alcohol, or pets. Women may eat. Women who need a bed call 211.
  • John 3:16 families and youth: 2027 Martin Luther King Jr Blvd, 918-592-1186. Call first.
  • City Rescue Mission, Oklahoma City: 405-232-2709. Women: 800 W California Ave, Mon–Fri 10am–3pm. Men: 914 W California Ave, Mon–Fri 9am–3pm.
  • Homeless Alliance day shelter: 1729 NW 3rd St, 7am–3pm daily, 405-415-8410. Winter overnight Nov 1 through Mar 31 at 1601 NW 4th St, 3pm–7am.
  • City Care night shelter: 532 N Villa Ave, 6pm–7am, 405-724-8439. Call first.
  • Norman day: Food and Shelter, 201 Reed Ave, 405-360-4954, day shelter 8am–4pm. Not a guaranteed bed.
  • Norman night: James Garner Ave and West Gray St, check-in 5pm, 405-652-1112.
  • Lawton Salvation Army: 1314 SW E Ave, 580-355-1802. Ask if a bed is open.
