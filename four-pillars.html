Raven's Nest Witches Co-op

A cooperative network for traditional orders, independent practitioners, teachers, and mystics working in direct synergy with WitchMart and Raven's Nest Temple.

Founding Allies

We proudly stand shoulder-to-shoulder with our founding allies, including the Temple of Hecate and the Altar of the Moon.

Standards of Alignment & Code of Ethics

Mission: Betterment, protection, and upliftment of humanity.

  • Zero Tolerance: selfishness, exploitation, gatekeeping for profit, or spiritual coercion.
  • Admin Veto Power: membership is a privilege, not a right. Raven's Nest Temple reserves the absolute right to review, deny, revoke, or ban any listing at any time.
  • Shared Safety Layer: A person at the Temple reviews every listing before it goes public.
  • Profit Donation Ask: If a listing makes a profit, we ask for a donation to the Temple so the work can continue. No set amount. Listings that do not make money are not asked.

Co-op Intake

Practitioners, teachers, and allied traditions can submit their profile for Temple review through the shared approval queue. We prioritize dignity, safety, consent, and community service in every alignment.
